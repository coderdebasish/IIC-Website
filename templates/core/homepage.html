WELCOME TO IEM UEM GROUP INNOVATION ECOSYSTEM

Driving Technological Innovation & Entrepreneurial Excellence in Eastern India

The Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM) and University of Engineering & Management (UEM) group is an acclaimed educational conglomerate amongst industry-centered academic institutions in India. For over three decades, IEM has set sublime standards in engineering education, managerial research, and technological incubation.

Established in accordance with the national directives of the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC), Govt. of India, the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) at IEM Kolkata operates as the primary engine for nurturing creative problem-solving, start-up incubation, design thinking, and intellectual property rights (IPR) awareness.

Through structured quarterly innovation calendars, National Innovation and Start-up Policy (NISP) integration, and hackathon series like the Smart India Hackathon (SIH), IIC IEM empowers undergraduate, postgraduate, and research scholars to transform academic concepts into commercial products and viable enterprises.

CORE DIVISIONS

Key Operational Thrust Areas

Systematic institutional mechanisms established to foster ideation, pre-incubation, intellectual property rights, and commercial startup ventures.

Ideation & Hackathons

Organizing periodic internal hackathons, Smart India Hackathon (SIH) internal screenings, and design thinking workshops to encourage prototype development.

Start-up & Pre-Incubation

Providing early-stage student enterprises with institutional co-working space, technical infrastructure, legal counsel, and seed investor pitching forums.

IPR & Patent Support Cell

Assisting student and faculty inventors with patent search, drafting, copyright registration, and intellectual property monetization guidance.

Industry & Alumni Mentorship

Connecting promising innovators with successful alumni founders, corporate venture capitalists, and industry experts for strategic guidance.

R&D & Prototyping Labs

State-of-the-art IoT, Robotics, AI, and Additive Manufacturing laboratories available round-the-clock for student prototype fabrication.

NISP & Policy Integration

Implementation of the National Innovation and Start-up Policy (NISP) framework allowing student founders academic flexibilities and IP ownership.
